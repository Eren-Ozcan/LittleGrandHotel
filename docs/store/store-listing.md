# Play Store Store Listing Content

You can copy-paste the following as-is into Play Console → "Store presence" →
"Main store listing". The character limits are Google's own limits; all of the
below are under the limit.

Two languages are kept here. **Turkish (`tr-TR`) is the listing's default
language** — that was a surprise found on 2026-08-19, and it means every one of
the 177 targeted countries was being shown a Turkish listing. English (`en-US`)
is a translation on top of it, added the same day. In Play Console a translation
lives under **Main store listing ▸ Manage translations ▸ Select languages**; a
locale with no graphics of its own inherits the default listing's icon, feature
graphic and screenshots, which is what `en-US` does.

**Keep the two in step.** The full descriptions below say the same things in the
same order, so a change to one is a change to both.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Little Grand Hotel is a relaxing idle management game where you grow your own little hotel from scratch into a starred chain.

🏨 BUILD YOUR HOTEL
Start with empty blocks, buy new rooms, and add facilities from guest rooms to a restaurant, from a pool to a spa. Place your rooms however you like, move them, and sell them if you need to.

🛋️ DECORATE, RAISE YOUR STARS
Furnish every room with furniture and decor, collect Style Score, and take your hotel from Basic to Iconic. The more stylishly you decorate, the higher your hotel's star rating climbs.

⏱️ MANAGE SHIFTS
Choose anything from a quick 1-hour round to a long 24-hour shift, and balance staff cost against profit margin. Thanks to automatic shift renewal, your hotel keeps producing even when you close the game.

🧹 CLEANING AND INFESTATIONS
Clean dirty rooms in time, or they turn into infestations! You can automate this job by building a Cleaning Room.

🚶 A LIVING HOTEL
Make your hotel feel truly alive with guests queuing outside the door, wandering around reception and riding the elevator up. Catch a runaway guest, poke a sleeping guest and earn a bonus.

🏆 QUESTS, ACHIEVEMENTS, PRESTIGE
A chain of 20 quests, 13 permanent achievements and a prestige system where you hand over your hotel at level 20 to earn a permanent income multiplier keep you in the game.

🎁 DAILY REWARDS AND OFFLINE EARNINGS
Log in every day and collect increasing rewards. When you close the game and come back, earnings for the time you spent offline are waiting for you too.

🌍 ENGLISH AND TURKISH
The whole game is playable in both languages, and you can switch between them at any time from Settings.

☁️ PLAYS OFFLINE, BACKED UP TO THE CLOUD
The game itself needs no connection — you can play it on a plane. Your progress is saved on your device and backed up to the cloud in the background; link a Google account and you can pick your hotel up on a new phone exactly where you left it. You decide what happens to your data: Settings has a single button that deletes the cloud copy and the local save together.

Download Little Grand Hotel and build your own little hotel empire!
```

## Graphic assets

Play Console requires the following:

- [x] **App icon**: 512×512 px, 32-bit PNG. Generated from `icon.svg` with
      `tools/gen_store_icon.gd` → `docs/store/icon_512.png`.
- [x] **Feature graphic**: 1024×500 px →
      `docs/store/feature_graphic_1024x500.png`.
- [x] **Phone screenshots**: `docs/store/screenshot_1_overview.png`,
      `docs/store/screenshot_2_lobby.png`.
- [x] **Category**: Games → Simulation.
- [x] **Content rating questionnaire**: filled in on Play Console (IARC).
- [x] **Data safety form**: submitted, and updated on 8 Aug 2026 to cover cloud
      save, the linked Google account and the deletion URLs.
- [x] **Privacy policy URL**: live at `https://yilkgames.com/privacy-policy/`
      and entered into the Play Console → App content → Privacy policy
      declaration (26 Jul 2026).
- [x] **Both listings entered in Play Console and submitted for review**
      (2026-08-19). Two changes went in together: the `tr-TR` full description
      was replaced (it still promised the old "carry your save with a code"
      flow, which cloud save replaced), and `en-US` was added from scratch —
      until then the app had no English listing at all. Play staged them under
      *Publishing overview*, they were sent to review, and — despite the
      dialog's "usually within 7 days" — they were published the same day
      (Publishing overview empty, last published date 19 Aug 2026).
- [ ] **Turkish screenshots** — the store screenshots carry English promise
