Last updated: 18 August 2026

Little Grand Hotel ("the app") is a free hotel management/idle game developed by
Yilk Games. This document explains what data the app processes.

## 1. Data we collect ourselves

Your game progress (coins, gems, room/decoration state, statistics) is stored
locally on your device. When you uninstall the app, this local data is deleted from
your device. From the Profile menu you can optionally turn your progress into a
text code (save code) and store/share it yourself; this code is not collected by us
and is not sent to any server.

The app also keeps a cloud backup of that progress so it is not lost if you lose
your device:

- On first run the app creates an anonymous account for you with Google's Firebase
  Authentication. This account is an identifier only — it contains no name, e-mail
  address or other personal detail, and you are not asked to register.
- A single save document is stored for that account in Google Cloud Firestore. It
  contains your game progress and a short summary of it (for example your coin
  count and hotel level), so that the same progress can be restored on another
  device. Purchase entitlements are never written into this document.
- If you choose "Sign in with Google" in the app (Profile ▸ cloud save), the same
  account is linked to your Google account so you can recover your progress on a
  new device. Through that sign-in we receive your account identifier and e-mail
  address; we do not receive your password.
- The document can only be read and written by that account itself; it is not
  shared with anyone and is not used for advertising.

## 2. Third-party services

The app uses these Google services:

- Google AdMob — used to show rewarded, interstitial and app-open ads. It may
  process data such as the advertising ID, device information and approximate
  location (IP-based) for ad personalization and measurement purposes. Users in the
  European Economic Area/United Kingdom and similar regions are shown a consent form
  through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any ads are displayed. You
  can reopen this form at any time from Settings ▸ Ad preferences.
- Google Play Billing — used to process in-app purchases (removing ads, income
  multiplier, gem packs). Payment and purchase information is processed directly by
  Google Play; card/payment information never reaches us.
- Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore — used only for the optional
  cloud save described in section 1.

For the data processing practices of these services, please see Google's own privacy
policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. Children's privacy

Little Grand Hotel is not specifically marketed to children and is aimed at a
general audience. Ads are served in the standard (non-child-directed) mode according
to the target audience settings.

## 4. Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes the data kept on your device. It does not delete the
cloud backup — use one of the following:

- In the app: Settings ▸ Delete account data. This deletes both this device's save
  and the cloud copy of it, together with the account record created for you.
- By e-mail: send a deletion request to yilkgamesstudio@gmail.com from the address
  you signed in with. We delete the account and its save document within 30 days.

We keep the cloud save document only while your account exists; there is no
separate backup retained after deletion.

To delete data processed through Google services (advertising ID, purchase
history), you can use your Google Account settings.
